## Phase 48: Add Little Indian Segment

**Goal:** Add Little Indian as the 8th gravel sector across all data sources, pipeline, map, and route explainer UI — visible on 100mi and 100k routes.

**Requirements:** SEG-01, SEG-02, SEG-03, SEG-04, DISP-01, DISP-02, DISP-03, PIPE-01, PIPE-02, FIX-01

**Success Criteria:**
1. Little Indian appears as an amber500 gravel overlay on the 100mi and 100k route maps between ND2225 and Doe Lake
2. Little Indian segment card displays in RouteExplainer with photo, 2-star difficulty, Strava link, and elevation sparkline
3. Clicking Little Indian on the map opens a sector detail panel with elevation data
4. Build pipeline completes without errors and generates correct annotations.json, sector-details.json, and sector-elevations.json for all 3 routes
5. SVG wave background in route explainer tiles seamlessly with no visible seam
