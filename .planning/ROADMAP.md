## Phase Details

### Phase 40: Map Simplification
**Goal**: Visitors see a clean, readable route map with gravel sectors visually distinct from other road — no confusing multi-color surface legend
**Depends on**: Nothing (first phase of v1.7)
**Requirements**: MAP-01, MAP-02
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Route track on the map displays exactly two colors — one for gravel sectors, one for all other road segments
  2. No surface-type legend or color key appears on or near the map
  3. The two-color scheme applies consistently across all three routes (100mi, 100k, 50k)
  4. Ghost polylines for inactive routes remain visible and visually subordinate

### Phase 42: Photo Pipeline
**Goal**: New photos are processed through the build pipeline and appear at their tagged locations on the map and in the gallery
**Depends on**: Nothing (independent of Phases 40-41)
**Requirements**: PHT-02, PHT-03, PHT-04
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. New photos have WebP thumbnails generated by the build pipeline at 400px/80% quality
  2. New photos appear as markers on the map at their assigned mileage points, clustered appropriately
  3. New photos appear in the gallery and open correctly in the PhotoSwipe lightbox
  4. Build pipeline completes without errors after adding the new photos

### Phase 43: Gallery & Layout Polish
**Goal**: Gallery and segment card layouts display photos at correct proportions and in route order
**Depends on**: Phase 42 (new photos must be in gallery before reordering)
**Requirements**: PHT-01, PHT-05, PHT-06
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Segment card photos in RouteExplainer display in a multi-column layout with each column no wider than 400px
  2. Gallery photos are ordered by route mileage from start to finish (not random or alphabetical)
  3. All thumbnails in gallery and segment cards preserve their original aspect ratios — no CSS stretching or distortion
  4. Multi-column segment card layout is responsive across mobile (375px), tablet (768px), and desktop (1280px)
