## Gaps

### Gap 1: EB Garamond Font Not Loading (VIS-02)

**Severity:** Medium — visual degradation, not functional break
**Root cause:** Font configured in `astro.config.ts` (lines 22-27) with `cssVariable: '--font-garamond'`, but `<Font cssVariable="--font-garamond" />` tag never added to `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro` `<head>`. Only Space Mono and National Park have `<Font>` tags.
**Effect:** `var(--font-garamond, 'EB Garamond', serif)` falls through both values to system serif (Times New Roman). Drop-caps render but in the wrong typeface.
**Fix:** Add `<Font cssVariable="--font-garamond" />` to BaseLayout.astro alongside the existing font tags. One-line fix.

### Gap 2: Backdrop Click Close (MAP-08)

**Severity:** Low — two of three close affordances work correctly
**Root cause:** `dialog.show()` (non-modal) was chosen deliberately over `showModal()` to keep the Leaflet map interactive while the panel is open. Non-modal dialogs have no native `::backdrop`. The `e.target === panel` handler only catches clicks on the dialog element's own padding.
**Effect:** Clicking the map area while panel is open does not close the panel. Users must use X button or Escape key.
**Fix options:**
  - A) Add a transparent full-viewport overlay behind the panel that calls closePanel() on click
  - B) Accept as deliberate trade-off (map interactivity > backdrop close) and document the decision
