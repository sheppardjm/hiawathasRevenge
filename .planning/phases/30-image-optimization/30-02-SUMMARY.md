## Decisions Made

- **No -webkit- prefix:** image-set() is Baseline widely available since September 2023; vendor prefix unnecessary and excluded per plan specification
- **type() notation over resolution descriptors:** format-based selection (type("image/webp")) is the correct approach for serving different image formats, not resolution-based (1x, 2x)
- **WebP first in image-set():** Browsers select the first supported format; WebP must precede JPEG to ensure modern browsers use the smaller file
