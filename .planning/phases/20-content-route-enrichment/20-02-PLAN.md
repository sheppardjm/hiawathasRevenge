  **Critical details:**
  - The pull quote uses `max-width: 48rem` which is wider than the `max-w-prose` (65ch ~ 40rem) parent container. The visual "breakout" is that the quote fills more horizontal space than the paragraph text. This works because the pull quote inherits the `max-w-4xl` outer container width (56rem), not the `max-w-prose` inner width.
  - Do NOT use negative margins, `width: 110%`, or viewport-breaking techniques — these cause horizontal scroll on mobile
  - The `width: 100%` ensures the quote fills available space up to `max-width: 48rem`
  - The `::before` pseudo-element creates the oversized left double quotation mark at 5rem (80px) — positioned absolutely so it doesn't affect text flow
  - `font-family: var(--font-display)` applies National Park typeface to the entire blockquote
  - `font-style: normal` overrides any inherited italic from the parent's `space-y-4` or blockquote defaults
  - The mobile breakpoint at 640px reduces padding and quotation mark size to prevent overflow
  - `background-color: var(--color-forest-950)` creates the color shift against the section's forest-900 background
  - ShieldMotif at `size={12}` renders as 12x24px (1:2 aspect ratio) — a subtle ornamental mark below the quote
  - `opacity: 0.4` on the ornament keeps it clearly decorative, not competing with the quote text
