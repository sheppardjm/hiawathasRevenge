## Notable Observations (Non-Breaking)

1. `content.config.ts` collections are hardcoded to `100mi/` paths — deliberate design choice for build-time default; runtime switching handled via fetch.
2. `stat-sectors` hardcoded to `7` at build time — correct for 100mi default, updated dynamically on route:change.
3. `copy-gpx.js` iterates `ROUTES` from `route-config.js` — adding a fourth route would be handled automatically.
