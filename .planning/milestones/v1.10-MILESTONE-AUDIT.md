### Threshold Change Regression Analysis

IntersectionObserver threshold changed from 0.15 → 0.01 in HiawathaExplainer.astro. This affects all 5 data-bg-fade elements (3 from Phase 47 History section + 2 new from Phase 49).

**Impact on Phase 47 History backgrounds:** Backgrounds fire slightly earlier (1% visible vs 15%). For normal-height divs (300-800px), this means the fade starts as the section edge enters the viewport rather than mid-scroll. No visual regression — backgrounds are present while reading content.

**Why necessary:** Gallery section at 5000+ px tall — 15% threshold requires 750+ px visible, exceeding typical viewport height. Observer would never fire.

**Verdict:** No regression.
